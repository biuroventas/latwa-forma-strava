Delete your Łatwa Forma account

Łatwa Forma (VENTAS NORBERT WRÓBLEWSKI) | Last updated: September 2026

You can delete your account and associated data in two ways. Account deletion removes your data; we do not merely freeze or deactivate the account.

1. In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Łatwa Forma app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

2. Email request (this page)

If you no longer have access to the app, send a request from the email address linked to your account:

Request account deletion

Contact: contact@latwaforma.pl. We will confirm and delete the data, typically within 30 days.

Premium subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play / App Store subscription. Cancel it first:

  • Android: Google Play → Subscriptions
  • iOS: Settings → [Your name] → Subscriptions
  • Website: Profile → Premium → Cancel subscription (Stripe)

What we delete

We delete your account and associated user data (profile, meals, activities, integrations). Records required by law (e.g. payment evidence) may be retained for the statutory period. Details: Privacy Policy.
