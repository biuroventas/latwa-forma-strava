Privacy Policy

Łatwa Forma | Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Controller

The data controller is VENTAS NORBERT WRÓBLEWSKI, ul. Szczytowa 27/10, 41-608 Świętochłowice, Poland, NIP 728-284-53-14. Contact: contact@latwaforma.pl.

2. Data We Collect

Łatwa Forma collects and processes data necessary for the app to function, including:

  • account data (email, user ID), including when signing in with Google – email and identifier from your Google account,
  • profile and fitness-related health data (age, gender, weight, height, activity level, body measurements) used only for BMR/TDEE calculations and goal tracking,
  • meal entries, activity logs, and water intake records,
  • meal photos – only if you use AI photo analysis; the photo is sent to the AI provider (OpenAI) to estimate nutrition and is not stored by us as a photo gallery,
  • AI advice questions – processed by OpenAI to generate a response,
  • when using integrations – access tokens for Strava and Garmin Connect, and imported activities,
  • for Premium subscription – user identifier passed to the payment operator to link the payment to your account: Stripe on the website; Apple / Google (and RevenueCat) in store apps. We do not store card data,
  • for local notifications (water, meal reminders) – a device identifier used by iOS/Android to deliver reminders.

3. Purpose of Processing

Data is used solely to provide the service: tracking calorie balance, nutrition, and physical activity, processing payments (Stripe on the website; Apple / Google / RevenueCat in mobile apps), AI features (meal photo analysis, nutrition advice), and sending notifications if you enable them. We do not sell data or use it for personalized ads.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (Art. 6 GDPR)

We process personal data on the following bases:

  • Performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – account data, profile data, meals, activities, water, integrations, and data necessary for Premium subscription, to provide the service under the terms of use.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) – push notifications (water, meal reminders) if you enable them; you can disable them in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – where necessary to assert or defend against claims, and to respond to your enquiries (e.g. contact@latwaforma.pl).

5. Google / Apple Sign-In and payments

Sign-in with Google or Apple is subject to those providers' policies; we only use email and identifier to provide the service.

Website: Premium payments are processed by Stripe – we pass your user identifier to link the payment to your account. Card data is processed by Stripe. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

iOS / Android apps: Premium payments use App Store / Google Play Billing via RevenueCat. See Apple, Google, and RevenueCat privacy policies.

5a. AI features (OpenAI) and product database

Meal photo analysis and AI advice use the OpenAI API. The photo or question is sent to OpenAI to generate a response. See OpenAI’s Privacy Policy. Calorie estimates and advice are not medical advice.

Barcode and name product lookup uses our database (including Turso) and the public Open Food Facts API. We do not send your health profile with those lookups.

The camera is used only when you choose to: scan a barcode (the image is not stored or uploaded) or take a meal photo (then the photo is sent to OpenAI as above).

6. Integrations (Strava, Garmin)

If you connect your Strava or Garmin Connect account, we import only the activities you have authorized. We do not share or sell this data to third parties. You can disconnect the integration at any time in the app settings.

Data from Garmin Connect is shared with us by you when you connect your account and is processed in accordance with Garmin Connect's privacy policy: Garmin Connect Privacy Policy.

7. Data Storage

Data is stored on Supabase servers (Europe). Integrated data (Strava, Garmin) is retained only as needed for synchronization.

Retention: Account-related data is kept until you delete your account (Profile → Delete account). After deletion, data is removed within the timeframe of our technical procedures (typically within 30 days). Data required for accounting (e.g. invoices, payment records) is kept for the period required by law (in Poland, including 5 years from the end of the tax year). Backup data may be deleted with some delay in line with our hosting policy.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure – “right to be forgotten” (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20 – on request we can provide your data in a format that allows transfer to another provider), and to object to processing (Art. 21). Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

You can delete your account and associated data in the app (Profile → Delete account) or via latwaforma.pl/delete-account.html (email contact@latwaforma.pl). Deletion is not account freezing. If you have a Google Play or App Store subscription, cancel it separately in the store – deleting the app account does not cancel it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Poland: President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw), uodo.gov.pl.

9. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for user tracking. If we use cookies necessary for the app to function (e.g. session), this will be stated in this policy.

10. Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page. By continuing to use the app after changes, you accept the updated policy.
